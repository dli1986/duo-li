## Overview

An automated pipeline for downloading YouTube videos and generating bilingual (Chinese/English) subtitles.

## Features

- Smart video download for YouTube and YouTube Kids, with retry and rate-limit handling
- Subtitle generation via OpenAI Whisper transcription plus an LLM (Ollama, OpenAI or Claude)
- Translation style adapts to video type — children's content, tech talks, interviews, documentaries
- Caching to avoid reprocessing already-downloaded videos
- Optional hardsub/softsub embedding, plus automatic upload to Bilibili
